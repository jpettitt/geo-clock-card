GeoClock Wallpaper for Mac

Your desktop becomes the live world map from geoclock.world — the real day/night terminator moving across NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble imagery, with ticking clocks for the places you care about. Free and open source.

Download GeoClock Wallpaper

v0.3.0-alpha · signed & notarized by Apple · macOS 13 or later · Intel & Apple Silicon

What it does

  • Live wallpaper on every display — the map re-renders on a schedule you pick, and the clock and location markers tick every second.
  • Location markers with day/night-aware colors, your home marker, an hour-of-day band, and time-zone overlays — the same options as the web version.
  • Multi-monitor aware, with optional per-display settings (different centering or fit per screen).
  • A menu-bar 🌍, no Dock icon. Pause, refresh, or open Settings from there.

Install

  1. Unzip the download and drag GeoClockWallpaper.app into Applications.
  2. Launch it — it appears as a 🌍 in the menu bar and paints the first wallpaper within a few seconds.
  3. Open Settings… from the menu-bar icon to add markers, change centering, or adjust the refresh cadence.

Want the screensaver too?

The GeoClock screensaver is a separate download — it isn't inside the app bundle above. The easy path: install the app first, then click Install screensaver… in Settings → Map. That sets everything up and takes you to the screensaver download. (The screensaver shows frames the app publishes, so it needs the app installed and running either way.)

Rather keep your own wallpaper?

Saver-only mode: turn off Show map on the desktop in Settings → Map and the app leaves your desktop alone, rendering only for the screensaver.
